1. Sequence - A list of numbers arranged in a specific order based on a pattern.

2. Arithmetic Progression (AP) - A sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms remains constant.

3. First Term - The starting value of an arithmetic progression, often denoted as 'a'.

4. Common Difference - The constant difference between consecutive terms in an arithmetic progression, denoted as 'd'.

5. nth Term Formula - A formula used to calculate the nth term of an arithmetic progression, given by \(a_n = a + (n-1)d\).

6. Number of Terms - The total count of terms in an arithmetic progression, usually represented by 'n'.

7. Sum of First n Terms - The total sum of the first n terms in an arithmetic progression, calculated using the formula \(S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n-1)d]\) or \(S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + a_n)\).

8. Last Term - The final term in a finite arithmetic progression, which can be found using the nth term formula.

9. Infinite Arithmetic Progression - A concept where an arithmetic progression continues indefinitely.

10. Application of Arithmetic Progression - Practical uses of arithmetic progression in real-life scenarios, such as calculating payments or analyzing sequences in nature. 

11. Relationship between Terms - Understanding how each term relates to previous terms in an arithmetic progression.

12. Finding Common Difference - The method to determine the common difference in an arithmetic progression when given specific terms.

13. Graphical Representation - Visualizing an arithmetic progression on a graph to understand its linear nature.

14. Reverse Calculation - Techniques to find earlier terms in an arithmetic progression from later terms.

15. Word Problems Involving AP - Solving real-world problems that can be modeled using arithmetic progressions. 

16. Characteristics of Arithmetic Progression - Identifying different properties and features that define an arithmetic progression. 

17. Convergence and Divergence - The concepts related to the behavior of sequences and whether they approach a limit or not in the context of arithmetic progressions. 

18. Comparison with Other Sequences - Understanding the differences and similarities between arithmetic progressions and other types of sequences, such as geometric progressions.
