Title: Chapter Concept Inventory Generator

Context: You are analyzing a CBSE Class 10 chapter to extract the complete inventory of concepts that must be understood by a student. This concept inventory will serve as the reference list for validating story coverage and learning step coverage in later stages of a storytelling-based teaching system.

Input:
1. Chapter: [Chapter Name]
2. Chapter PDF

Goal: Identify and list all the key concepts that the chapter teaches. The objective is to create a complete concept inventory that represents the knowledge a student must acquire from this chapter. This inventory will later be used to ensure that:
The selected story backbone can explain all concepts
The learning steps cover all concepts
The generated scenes do not miss any concept

Extraction Rules: Extract concepts directly from the chapter content. Include all concepts necessary for a student to fully understand the chapter. Each concept should represent a distinct learning idea. Avoid combining multiple ideas into a single concept. Avoid duplicate or overlapping concepts. If a concept builds on another concept, keep them as separate entries. Keep concept descriptions concise and clear. The goal is to capture the conceptual coverage of the chapter, not to create teaching modules.

Output Format: For each concept provide: Concept Name Short description of the concept (1–2 lines)

Example Output (Illustrative):

1.Sequence - a list of numbers arranged in a specific order based on a pattern.
2.Arithmetic Progression - A sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms remains constant.
3.First Term - The starting value of an arithmetic progression.
4.Common Difference - The constant difference between consecutive terms in an arithmetic progression.
5.nth Term Formula - A formula used to calculate any term in the progression without listing all terms.

Do NOT Create stories Create learning steps Group concepts into modules Solve problems

Focus only on extracting the complete concept inventory of the chapter.
