Title: Chapter Decomposition Engine for the Selected Story Backbone

Context: I am teaching a CBSE Class 10 student using a selected real-world story as the narrative backbone. The goal is to transform the chapter into a sequence of learning steps that unfold naturally within the chosen story, so that the student experiences the chapter as a smooth and engaging narrative.

Input:

Chapter:
(Insert the chapter name used in previous prompts)

Selected Real-World Story Narrative:
(Insert the selected story backbone output from the previous prompt — specifically the **Core Narrative Premise** of the chosen story)

Concept Inventory:
(Insert the **Concept Inventory output generated from Prompt 0**)

Goal:
Analyze the chapter and decompose it into logically coherent learning steps that together explain the entire chapter in a clear and engaging manner.
The objective is not to restrict the number of steps, but to ensure that:
	Pedagogical clarity is preserved
	The conceptual flow of the chapter is maintained
	No conceptual dependency is broken
	The story backbone can naturally carry the explanation
	The student can follow the story and understand the concepts smoothly
	The number of learning steps should emerge naturally from the structure and the flow of the chapter and the narrative flow of the story.

Decomposition Rules:
Each learning step should represent one meaningful stage in the student's understanding of the chapter.
A concept may require multiple sequential learning steps if that improves clarity and narrative continuity.
Closely related ideas may remain together only if they can be clearly understood within a single learning step.
If a concept requires staged explanation, split it into multiple steps instead of compressing it.
Avoid duplicate learning steps.
If two steps cover overlapping conceptual ground, merge them or clearly differentiate them.
Ensure that all concepts from the chapter are covered at least once.
Do not skip any essential concept.
Avoid unnecessary repetition of the same concept across multiple steps unless the later step extends or deepens the idea.
Maintain conceptual progression where appropriate:
	Intuition
	Structural understanding
	Formula derivation (if applicable)
	Application or reinforcement

Ensure each learning step corresponds to a natural moment in the selected story backbone where the concept can be discovered, observed, or explained.
Ensure that each learning step logically leads to the next, avoiding conceptual jumps that would break narrative continuity.
Each learning step should be expandable into multiple scenes later.

Output Format:
For each learning step provide:
Learning Step ID
Learning Step Title
Concepts Introduced
Bullet points listing the concepts explained in this step

Narrative Moment:
A detailed description of minimum 5+ lines covering:
Story Context
Trigger Event
Character Interaction
Concept Discovery Setup
Transition to Next Learning Step

Concept Coverage Validation:
After generating all learning steps, provide a Concept Coverage Table.
For each concept from the Concept Inventory provide:
Concept → Learning Step(s) where it appears → Coverage Completeness → Coverage %
Coverage Completeness Allowed Values:
	Fully Covered
	Partially Covered
	Requires Additional Step

Coverage % indicates how clearly the concept is represented across the learning steps.

Do NOT:
Create scenes
Write story dialogue
Provide detailed teaching explanations
Solve textbook problems

Focus only on building a clear sequence of learning steps that combines the chapter structure with the story backbone in a natural and understandable flow.
